# About

I grew up in Turnhout, a town in the north of Belgium best known for making playing cards, and
have since lived in Leuven, London, Amsterdam and Ghent. In Leuven I studied physics and
philosophy at the same time, which sounds more coherent than it felt: one degree ended in a
thesis defending panpsychism, the other in working out how much energy a gravitational wave
carries. Amsterdam was two years of listening for echoes in LIGO data, on the off chance that
black holes have a surface. They probably don't.

When the pandemic started I joined the modelling group at Ghent University and spent a year
building spatial epidemic models that fed into the Belgian government's advice. That turned
into a PhD, joint between Ghent and the University of São Paulo, on cellular automata and
network automata: iterate a simple local rule on a grid or a network and see what comes out.
Spoiler: almost anything. Five years, eight peer-reviewed papers, and a defence on 2 October 2026.

Along the way I taught calculus and differential equations to a few hundred bioscience
engineers, and loved it. I wrote popular-science pieces in Dutch, co-organised ACRI 2026 in
Ghent, the leading international conference on cellular automata, and, through two persuasive
friends and a stack of books, found Effective Altruism. That stuck: I sit on the board of EA
Ghent, and I've made up my mind that AI safety is the most pressing problem of our generation.
Right after the defence I head to San Francisco for a deep dive into theoretical alignment,
then a hackathon or two, the BlueDot project sprint, and with luck a fellowship. The plan is to
get deep into the technical side first and then find the most effective way to support
technical AI governance from Brussels. [Here is what I mean by that](/ai-safety/).

I adore the maths, the code and the philosophy, but what really gets me going is building
things with people: EA lectures, musical performances, nights out, many of them at
[BHL440](/ventures/), the eight-person co-housing I bought and founded in 2021 and still run.
When I'm not in front of a screen I'm in the [mountains](/mountains/) or behind a pair of
turntables playing [jungle and tekno](/dj/) as Brak Geboren.
