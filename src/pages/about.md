# About

I grew up in Belgium and studied physics and philosophy in Leuven at the same time, which
sounds more coherent than it felt. The philosophy ended in a thesis defending panpsychism; the
physics continued in Amsterdam, where I spent two years looking for echoes of gravitational
waves in LIGO data with the Nikhef group.

When the pandemic started I joined the modelling group at Ghent University and spent a year
building spatial epidemic models that fed into the Belgian government's advice. That turned
into a PhD, joint between Ghent and the University of São Paulo, on cellular automata and
network automata: what happens when you iterate a simple local rule on a grid or a network,
and how you can tell the resulting behaviours apart. I handed it in in 2026.

Along the way I taught calculus and differential equations to a few hundred bioscience
engineers, wrote popular-science pieces in Dutch, co-organised an international conference on
cellular automata in Ghent, and got involved in Effective Altruism. The last one stuck. I sit
on the board of EA Ghent, and I'm now looking for work in AI safety.
[Here is what I mean by that](/ai-safety/).

When I'm not in front of a screen I'm in the [mountains](/mountains/), behind a pair of
turntables playing [jungle and tekno](/dj/), or fixing something at [BHL440](/ventures/),
the co-housing I started in 2021.
