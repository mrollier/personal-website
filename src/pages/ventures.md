# Ventures

## BHL440

A co-housing community in Ghent that I started in 2021 and still run. That means the boring
parts: contracts, plumbing, the shared budget, and the occasional house meeting that runs long.
It is also the best decision I made that year.

## Effective Altruism Ghent

Since 2025 I'm on the board of EA Ghent, where I host and co-organise workshops and lectures:
*Powerful AI is coming. How do we get it right?*, *Your Career as a Force for Good*, and
*An Evening on Effective Giving*. I'm also an ambassador for
[Effectief Geven](https://effectiefgeven.be/), the Belgian GiveWell-style charity advisor.

## Music events

- **Pinnacle, Leuven** (2014–2019): founded, managed, and played resident at a series of industrial techno nights.
- **Zenevloed, Amsterdam** (2017–2019): organised and managed experimental electronic music events.
- Self-employed [DJ](/dj/) since 2012 at parties, raves, record stores and radio stations, including Kompass Klub, Urgent.fm and Red Light Radio.
